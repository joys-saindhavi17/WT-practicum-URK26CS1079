Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must satisfy the required academic qualifications to apply for admission.

  1. Candidate must have completed 12th standard.
  2. Required subjects must be studied in 12th standard.
  3. Candidate must meet the minimum percentage requirement.
  4. Candidate must satisfy the university admission rules.
